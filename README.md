# Chronaxis installers

Official installers for **Chronaxis**: EOT claims and progress reports from your Primavera P6 programmes, on your own computer.

**Download page: https://www.chronaxisapp.com/download** (English) · https://www.chronaxisapp.com/ar/download (العربية)

Every download starts with a 14-day free trial with every feature unlocked. No card needed.

## Which file do I need?

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `Chronaxis_<version>_Windows_x64_Setup.exe` |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 12 or later | `Chronaxis_<version>_macOS_AppleSilicon.dmg` |

The latest files are always under **Releases** on the right.

## First launch

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS:** drag Chronaxis to Applications and open it. If macOS says it cannot check the app, go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

Your system asks only once. Code-signed installers are on the way.

## Privacy

Your programmes, contracts and letters are processed on your computer and never uploaded.
